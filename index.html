E Susu Scheme Terms & Conditions

Please read all terms carefully before accepting. These rules govern Christabel Susu Hub contributions and payouts.

Rule 1

Daily Payment Deadline (6:30 p.m.)

All members are required to make their agreed Susu contributions by 6:30 p.m. each day, where daily contributions apply. Members must strictly adhere to the 1-week contribution schedule agreed upon with the administrator to protect group payout predictability.

Rule 2

Penalties for Delay (GH₵50 on Second Delay)

Members are expected to make contributions and meet their agreed payment obligations on time.

  • First Delay: An official warning reminder is issued to settle overdue contributions immediately.
  • Second Delay: A mandatory GH₵50 late penalty applies upon the second qualifying delay according to the agreed rules of the Susu scheme.
  • A delay is defined as failure to submit agreed funds by 6:30 p.m. or delaying an agreed cashout arrangement without 24 hours prior administrative notice.
Rule 3

Payout Slot Reassignment

If a member delays for a third time and can no longer maintain the agreed contribution schedule, the administrator may reassign the member's payout slot to the back of the queue.

Example: A member in slot 1 may be moved to the end of the group queue, while an on-time member is prioritized, provided the revised arrangement is consistent with scheme rules. Any slot reassignment must be communicated in writing to the affected members.

Rule 4

Early Withdrawal or Opting Out

If a member is unable to continue after the scheme has started, the member may request to opt out by notifying the administrator.

The administrator will calculate the agreed administrative deduction from the amount the member has contributed and refund the remaining balance. The administrator will disclose the exact administrative deduction percentage and refund schedule prior to processing.

Rule 5 & 6

Member Acceptance & Privacy Notice
